About Rabbi Favish Dalfin

Rabbi Favish Mordechai Dalfin and Chaya Sara moved from Brooklyn to Florida in 1990 as shluchim of the Lubavitcher Rebbe. In 2003 they established Ohr Menachem Chabad in North Bay Village and founded Simcha Connection.

Simcha Connection brings food, clothing, emergency help, and simcha to widows, orphans, and families in crisis — in Florida and in Israel (Elad branch, 2007). Mrs. Chaya Sara Dalfin is principal of the girls' division at LEC Chabad in North Miami Beach.

RFD.com is the home for his video clips, transcripts, and a direct path to partner in the chesed.
